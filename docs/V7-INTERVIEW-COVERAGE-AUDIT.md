# V7 frozen-build interview coverage audit

The release contains 169 available lessons: 142 new and 27 inherited. Eight capstones (SP-093–100) are deferred at the user’s request. All 235 source questions were individually reviewed. This maps bounded practice, not complete vendor products or the learner’s work history.

| Classification | Questions |
| --- | ---: |
| personal-response | 82 |
| verified-practice | 36 |
| partial-practice | 87 |
| conceptual-only | 15 |
| not-implemented | 15 |

`verified-practice` requires implemented, tested outcomes in cited available lessons. `partial-practice` identifies a useful subset and its limits. `conceptual-only` and `not-implemented` do not count as executable coverage. `personal-response` covers behavioral, employment and interview logistics that the learner must answer truthfully. Zero unreviewed questions does not mean zero unsupported questions.

Difficulty is the highest referenced exercise level, not candidate seniority. The [machine-readable mapping](V7-INTERVIEW-QUESTION-COVERAGE.json) preserves exact source wording, available/deferred lessons, actual suitable environments, version-pinned lesson references and fixture test names. Existing scenario associations are provenance, not independently verified coverage. Inherited lessons retain workflow-based completion semantics; their compatibility tests do not establish full technical outcome coverage.

[Release evidence](V7-RELEASE-TEST-EVIDENCE.json) cross-checks all lesson acceptance names against passed assertions from the final regression. IQ054 additionally cites the existing employee CSV overwrite/append/replay test. The [release audit](V7-FROZEN-RELEASE-AUDIT.md) records broader feature limits and checks.
